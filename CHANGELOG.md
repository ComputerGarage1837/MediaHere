# Changelog

All notable changes to MediaHere are listed here. The section for each
version is shown to users inside the app when an update is available.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-05

### Added
- MediaHere: a new look and name. Black theme with green accents, an aurora hero on the home
  screen, new logo and app icon, and the tagline "Good Entertainment Lives Here".
- Everything from the previous Android TV / Fire TV / phone client: Live TV and Video on Demand
  with their own sign-ins, TV guide, multi-view, downloads and recordings, resume playback and
  in-app updates (now served from this repository).
